About us

Engineering excellence, since 2014.

Wahat Al Hadaf Technical Services LLC was founded in Dubai in 2014 to deliver engineering excellence in the civil and electromechanical fields. Today we are a facilities management and MEP contractor trusted for workmanship, fair pricing and results.

Our mission is to be the one-contact solution, any time. A loyal, experienced team — backed by our trading division’s links with leading brands — means quality products and dependable work on every project.
