Air conditioning
Filter cleaning, gas and drain checks, duct cleaning.
Electrical
Panels, sockets, lighting and fault finding.
Plumbing
Leaks, water heaters, pumps and drainage.
General upkeep
Painting touch-ups, carpentry, doors and locks.
Optional add-ons
Weekly pool care and scheduled garden maintenance.

Questions we hear often

How do I get a quote?

Call, WhatsApp or send the form on our contact page. We arrange a site visit, then send a written, itemised quote before any work starts.

Can I combine several services?

Yes. That’s the point of one team: for example a pool renovation with new interlock around it and fresh landscaping, managed as one job.

Do you supply the materials?

Yes. Our trading division works with leading brands, so we can supply tiles, kitchen units, pool equipment and more as part of the job.

Can pool and garden care be part of an AMC?

Yes. Both can be added to a maintenance contract so the whole property is covered under one plan.

Where do you work?

We are based in Port Saeed, Deira, Dubai. Tell us where your property is and we’ll confirm.
